contacts rather than through external recruitment — reported by the researcher

## What this does NOT change

The pre-registered **inclusion criterion is untouched**: *has personally run
paid ads, or personally reads ad performance numbers, in the last 12 months.*
A convenience sampling frame is not a relaxation of eligibility. Contacts are
screened with S1/S2 exactly as before; anyone included despite failing S1 is a
declared, dated deviation (`evidence/kpi6-sus/protocol.md` §2, §8).

The exclusion criterion matters *more* under this frame, not less: friends are
the population most likely to have already seen the app. S2 is asked, never
assumed.
