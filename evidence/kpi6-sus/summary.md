## Task measures — descriptive, no threshold was pre-registered

Full sample — eligibility is unattributable in this run. `docs/KPI_SPEC.md` deliberately pre-registers no bar
for these; inventing one now would be the HARKing the spec exists to prevent.
Time-on-task counts successful attempts only — an abandoned task contributes its
failure, not a 300-second time that would flatter or punish the median.

**Not measured. 11 of 11 sessions recorded no task outcome**, and
no table is printed, because a table assembled from participants who were never
scored would describe an empty set while looking like a result.

The tasks *were* performed — participants worked through T1-T5 against the
production build before filling in the SUS, which is what the protocol requires
for the SUS to be about the system rather than about a description of it. What
is missing is the facilitator's record of how each one went.

**Consequence, stated plainly.** SUS measures perception. The objective leg —
did they actually succeed, how long did it take, how often were they unblocked —
is absent, so this study cannot say whether a participant who rated the system
highly could in fact complete its core tasks. The methodology chapter states this
as a limitation of the study, not as a property of the system.
